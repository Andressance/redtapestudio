Red Tape Studios is an independent game development studio specializing in satirical simulations. We build deeply systemic games that explore the absurdities of everyday bureaucracy and authority.

Our Philosophy

We believe the most compelling gameplay often hides in the most unexpected places. By taking notoriously tedious real-world systems and injecting them with dark humor and profound, systemic mechanics, we craft unique satirical experiences that challenge and entertain.

In Development

Our current portfolio focuses on simulations that explore the human condition through the lens of strict protocols, endless paperwork, and moral ambiguity.

Active Development (Alpha)

Project 'Audit'

A complex management simulator exploring the intricate and often absurd world of personal finance and bureaucratic compliance. Navigate a labyrinth of rules, enforce systemic logic, and deal with unexpected comedic consequences.

Pre-Production

Project 'Citation'

A civic authority simulator focusing on vehicular law enforcement. Balance public order, manage shifting regulations, and navigate the moral gray areas of daily transit in a highly reactive sandbox environment.

Our Approach

Systemic Design

Emergent Gameplay

Our core philosophy revolves around creating robust, interacting systems rather than heavily scripted events. This allows for unparalleled player agency and highly replayable, dynamic scenarios.

Narrative

Interactive Satire

We use sharp writing and environmental storytelling to highlight the humor in strict rulesets. The narrative emerges naturally from the player's interactions with our complex, often stubborn, bureaucratic simulations.

Pipeline

AI-Powered Workflow

As an agile independent studio, we leverage advanced Large Language Models to scale our capabilities. From procedural NPC dialogue generation to validating complex economic systems, AI is at the core of our development pipeline.
